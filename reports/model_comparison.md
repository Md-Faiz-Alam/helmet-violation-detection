## Decisions

**2026-11-21: `yolo26s_640_oversample`** won the first 7 experiments on val (mAP50 0.925, no-helmet recall 0.843, +0.032 / +0.034 over the next model).

**2026-11-22: `yolo26s_640_headwear` promoted** to `models/best.pt`.
- On our val set the three YOLO26s + oversampling variants are within noise (mAP50 0.916-0.925); the headwear models raise val no-helmet recall 0.843 → 0.887 / 0.896.
- The evidence for the extra data comes from held-out sets that were never used for choosing: headwear test mAP50 0.320 → 0.810 ([model_comparison_headwear.md](model_comparison_headwear.md)), video violation recall 5/12 → 8/12 at 100% precision ([video_eval.md](video_eval.md)).
- The two headwear runs tie on val (0.918 vs 0.916 mAP50), so the shorter training run was kept.
