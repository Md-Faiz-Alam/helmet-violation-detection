## Decision (2026-11-21)

**Winner: `yolo26s_640_oversample`**, promoted to `models/best.pt`.

- Best on both validation metrics, by a margin larger than the noise of a 126-image val set: val mAP50 0.925 (+0.032 over the next model) and val no-helmet recall 0.843 (+0.034).
- Same CPU speed as the other small models (about 108 ms/img), smaller than YOLOv8s (9.9M vs 11.1M params).
- `yolo26s_640` has a slightly higher test no-helmet recall (0.885 vs 0.869, one object out of 61). Test is not used for selection, and that gap is noise.
- All runs trained the full 50 epochs with their best epoch between 41 and 48, so a longer run of the winner is a cheap follow-up.
- If CPU speed matters more than accuracy (for example video on a laptop), `yolov8s_416` and the nano models run about 2× faster for roughly 0.07 lower val mAP50.
